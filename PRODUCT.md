# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solo founder at the keyboard, moving between Workspace home, a Project, and Capture. They already know Linear-class tools; they will not tolerate a skeleton or a second dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Cantiara is a personal project operating system. This surface gets the founder into the Workspace, into one Project Overview, or into Capture — without inventing Work, widgets, or a second Workspace.

## Brand Personality

Exact, quiet, durable.

## Anti-references

Mission Control health scores, widget dashboards, cream SaaS landings, Better-T-Stack ASCII home, twin Sign In CTAs, blue raw links as primary actions, overlapping theme icons.

## Design Principles

- One job per surface: Workspace home lists Projects; Project opens Overview; Capture is its own route.
- Earned familiarity: chrome, list, and primary button should disappear into the task.
- Hierarchy by type and space, not cards or color.
- Honest empty: missing product areas stay empty; they do not fake Kanban or Work.
- English UI; glossary names only.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard and screen reader for create, Configuration Mode, and navigation. Contrast ≥4.5:1 for body. `prefers-reduced-motion` on any motion. Focus rings from the existing button system.
