# Geri Bildirim Kaydı ve Feed

## Problem Statement

Kurucu gelen özgün mesajı kanal, zaman ve kimlik bağlamıyla kaybetmeden tutmak ve uzun içeriği yoğun okumak ister. Bugün Geri Bildirim Kaynak alt türüne, özellik isteğine veya destek ticket'ına kayabilir; özet aslının yerine geçebilir; İşe dönüşüm kaydı silebilir; feed Bildirim Merkezi veya sosyal yaşam döngüsü gibi davranabilir. Contact birleştirme ve kişisel veri silme bu kartın işi değildir. Kaynak tazeliği 44'tedir.

## Solution

Kurucu Geri Bildirimi ayrı uzman ana kayıt olarak tutar: özgün mesaj, kanal, zaman, isteğe bağlı bağlantı ve ekler, isteğe bağlı Contact/Company, ve proje/İş/Karar bağları. Özet veya Kanıt niteliği aslının yerine geçmez. Kayıt özellik isteği, İş veya sosyal gönderi değildir. Kurucu mesajı önizlemeyle tam olarak bir İşe dönüştürebilir; Geri Bildirim silinmez. Feed aynı ana kayıtlardan yoğun okuma yüzeyidir; beğeni, yorum dizisi veya sosyal yaşam yoktur ve Kaynak kaydının sürüm sözleşmesini yeniden tanımlamaz.

## User Stories

1. As a founder, I want Feedback as its own expert master record, so that an incoming message is not a Source subtype and does not inherit URL recheck or snapshot life.
2. As a founder, I want the original message, channel, time, optional link, and attachments preserved, so that later summary cannot replace what was said.
3. As a founder, I want optional Contact and Company on Feedback, so that identity is available when I know the sender and not invented when I do not.
4. As a founder capturing Feedback from an unknown sender, I want the product not to force Contact creation, so that identity stays honest.
5. As a founder, I want multiple Feedback records to bind to the same Work as separate origins, so that several messages do not collapse into one vote.
6. As a founder, I want Feedback never to be a vote, automatic priority, or roadmap instruction, so that a message cannot steer planning by count.
7. As a founder converting Feedback to Work, I want a preview of the one Work to create, starting title/body mapping, and the `Kökeni` relation, so that nothing is born until I confirm.
8. As a founder after that conversion, I want the Feedback record to remain, so that converting is not deleting the original.
9. As a founder, I want conversion to create exactly one master record and never a batch of hidden records, so that one message is not silently exploded.
10. As a founder, I want Feedback statuses `New`, `Reviewed`, and `Archived` not to change related Work status, so that reading a message is not a planning mutation.
11. As a founder attaching Kanıt niteliği on a Work–Feedback link, I want reported problem, suggested solution, workaround, impact, frequency, independence, and audience fit kept as separate optional fields, so that missing values do not block the relation.
12. As a founder, I want severity, frequency, independence, or audience fit that were not in the source labeled as my interpretation with author and time, so that commentary is not presented as the original message.
13. As a founder, I want the product never to extract those fields from the message or declare campaign siblings as unique evidence, so that independence stays a human judgment.
14. As a founder, I want Kanıt niteliği on one Work–Feedback link not to copy silently onto another Work, so that the same message can mean different context per target.
15. As a founder, I want Kanıt Rolü on that link to stay a separate field from Kanıt niteliği, so that direction of use is not derived from impact fields and forms no combined score.
16. As a founder, I want optional follow-up `Follow up`, `Followed up`, or `Outcome verified` on the evidence link only, so that tracker intent does not become Feedback or Work lifecycle or a requester CRM.
17. As a founder in the first product, I want Feedback created from in-app Quick Capture and existing sources, so that there is no public form, comment, vote, or two-way requester thread.
18. As a founder, I want a dense Feed of Feedback and long-body Source records showing identity or channel, time, attachments, project, and related Work/Decision, so that I can scan originals without a second record type.
19. As a founder, I want a Feed row not to be a social post, comment thread, vote, or second status lifecycle, so that sorting the Feed cannot change Source or Feedback status or priority.
20. As a founder opening a Feed row, I want `Open Source Record` to the same master record, so that the Feed is a reading surface rather than a copy.
21. As a founder, I want the Feed to reuse Smart Collection filter and sort conditions, so that scanning does not invent a second query language.
22. As a founder, I do not want the Feed to be the Unified Notification Center, inbox product, or customer-support tool, so that attention signals stay in their own feature.
23. As a founder, I do not want the Feed to redefine Source versioning or recheck, so that freshness stays in the Source feature.
24. As a founder, I want English UI `Feedback`, `Feed`, `Convert to Work`, and `Open Source Record`, so that labels match the term table.
25. As a founder using only a keyboard or a screen reader, I want to record Feedback, convert with preview, and scan the Feed, so that the evidence journey is possible without a pointer.
26. As a founder, I do not want likes, comment threads, or social lifecycle on Feedback, so that the first product stays an identity-and-evidence book.
27. As a founder, I do not want this feature to merge Contacts or erase personal data, so that 46 and 81 stay the owners of those actions.
28. As a founder, I do not want Feedback to be treated as a Source, özellik isteği, Work record, support ticket, social post, or CRM opportunity, so that the glossary Avoid list holds.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Owning documents.** Behavior is owned by [Geri Bildirim Kaydı](../../prd/08-search-relations-and-evidence.md#geri-bildirim-kaydı) and [Geri Bildirim ve Kaynak Feed görünümü](../../prd/08-search-relations-and-evidence.md#geri-bildirim-ve-kaynak-feed-görünümü). Lifecycle is [ana kayıt türleri](../../prd/02-domain-model-and-lifecycle.md#ana-kayıt-türleri-ve-asgari-sözleşmeler) (`Yeni` / `İncelendi` / `Arşivlendi`). Origin and participant relations are [standart ilişki türleri](../../prd/02-domain-model-and-lifecycle.md#standart-ilişki-türleri). Kanıt Rolü as a field on the link is defined in [Kanıt Rolü](../../prd/08-search-relations-and-evidence.md#kanit-rolu-ve-iliski-ustverisi) and rendered in Kanıt Akışı by 45; this feature stores Feedback-specific niteliği and follow-up on the Work–Feedback link and must not build Kanıt Akışı. Capture channels are [Yakalama](../../prd/05-capture-and-intake.md). No new ADR.
- **Glossary.** Use Geri Bildirim, Contact, Company, Kaynak, İş, Kanıt bağı, Kökeni, Kanıt niteliği, Kanıt Rolü, Birleşik Bildirim Merkezi (must not be this Feed). Do not introduce Source-record subtype, social post, support ticket, CRM opportunity, inbox product, or a second Feed record.
- **Master record.** Feedback is a Project-scoped expert master record. It keeps original message, channel, time, optional URL, attachments, optional Contact (`Katılımcısı`) and Company, and relations to Project, Work, and Decision. It does not inherit Source URL recheck, candidate snapshot, or version life. Creating Feedback from Quick Capture or an existing Source copies origin; it does not turn Feedback into that Source.
- **Unknown sender.** Contact is optional. Write paths must not require creating a Contact to save Feedback.
- **Not a vote.** Many Feedback records may origin-link to one Work. Count, uniqueness, or independence is never an automatic priority or roadmap instruction. Unique Contact/Company counts shown on Work priority supports (PRD 06) are context, not this feature's scoring engine.
- **Convert to Work.** `Convert to Work` previews exactly one Work, target Project, starting title/body from the original message, and the `Kökeni` relation. Confirm creates that Work; the Feedback record is not deleted or archived by conversion. No AI, no multi-record spawn, no skipping Work field rules. This is not Test Handoff and not a support ticket close.
- **Niteliği vs original.** Work–Feedback evidence may carry optional Kanıt niteliği fields, each blank or `Unknown`. Values not explicit in the source are labeled as founder interpretation with author and time. The original message is shown as a separate text. The product does not extract fields from the message. The same Feedback may carry different niteliği per Work; one link does not copy onto another. Kanıt Rolü on the same link is a separate optional field; neither is derived from the other; they form no score. Optional `Follow up` / `Followed up` / `Outcome verified` is link-local tracker intent only.
- **Statuses.** Feedback `New` / `Reviewed` / `Archived` do not write Work status, priority, or Kanban membership.
- **Feed.** Feed is a dense reading view over the same Feedback and long-body Source master records, with identity or channel, time, attachments, project, and related Work/Decision. It may reuse Smart Collection filter/sort. A row is not a record, not a social post, not a thread, not a vote, and not a second lifecycle. Order does not write source status or priority. Detail opens via `Open Source Record`. Feed is not Unified Notification Center, not universal search, and not an inbox product. It does not redefine Source versioning (44).
- **First-product intake.** In-app Quick Capture and existing sources only. No public form, comments, votes, or two-way requester conversation.
- **English UI labels.** `Feedback`, `Feed`, `Convert to Work`, `Open Source Record`, `New`, `Reviewed`, `Archived`, `Follow up`, `Followed up`, `Outcome verified`. Missing labels join the PRD term table in the same change. No Turkish UI.
- **Classification.** Private Feedback bodies are hassas kişisel veri. Original message is not replaced by summary in export or share previews this feature might show; sharing itself is 73.

- **Minimal Research Session intake (#329).** `packages/db/src/schema/feedback.ts` and `apps/server/src/features/feedback/server/feedback-database.ts` provide original message, channel, occurrence time, title, Project and `New` status for exactly one confirmed research conversion. The intake is invoked only inside the consumer’s authorized atomic transaction; its reader scopes originals to the Project owner. Contact is optional and no identity is invented. Feed, Quick Capture, lifecycle editing, niteliği/follow-up and Feedback → Work remain later deliveries.

- **Research Evidence record opening (#329 review).** Research Sessions can explicitly open a linked native Feedback record through an owner-scoped read-only collaborator. Archived Feedback remains readable in that dialog; the historical Evidence row itself contains no old target title/body. The caller validates the source's current Consent, binding identity and both ends' workspace access. Failed or pending reads hide cached bodies. This supplies `Open source record` on the consuming Evidence surface without introducing Feed, classification or Feedback editing. Testing Decisions: the consuming seam evidence below.

- **Feedback master record (#360).** Feedback is created in the Project's Discovery area by `Create Feedback` (`Title`, `Original message`, `Channel`, `Occurred at`, `Link`, `Attachments`) and still from Research Sessions. `Link` is the optional `project_feedback.url` (http/https only, no recheck or snapshot). `Attachments` are references (`project_feedback_attachment`) to existing active Project-scoped File Attachments; Feedback owns no file bytes or versions. The contract in `packages/api/src/feedback.ts` is strict: a summary, Contact, Source type, Work id/status, priority or Kanban field is rejected. The status command (`New` / `Reviewed` / `Archived`, optional `expectedRevision`) carries only a status and its PostgreSQL adapter writes only `project_feedback`; it never reads or writes Work. Archived Projects are read-only. Feed remains #363 and Kanıt niteliği is #362.

- **Optional identity and Convert to Work (#361).** `Contact (optional)` and `Company (optional)` pick existing Workspace identity records, default to `Unknown`, and Feedback saves without either; no Contact is ever created from Feedback. The Contact link reuses `workspace_contact_feedback` (the `Katılımcısı` link Contact merge already moves), the single Company uses `workspace_company_feedback` (migration `0118`, primary key on the Feedback). `setFeedbackIdentity` sets or clears both and carries nothing else; archived or foreign-Workspace identity is refused. `Convert to Work` opens a dialog: `Work type` (`Task` default, `Bug`, `Research`, `Improvement`; not `Feature`), `Preview` (read-only: exactly one Work, `Target Project` (the Feedback's own Project), `Title` = Feedback title, `Description` = verbatim original message, the `Origin` relation) and `Confirm`. `buildFeedbackWorkPlan` in `packages/api/src/feedback.ts` is the only mapping; Work field rules are the Work Lifecycle title/description/type schemas, and the Work is inserted by `insertWorkWithKeyAllocation` (shared with Document `Convert to new record and bind`) under the Project lock. Confirm runs through the Mutation Contract with a stable idempotency key: the plan is re-derived under the Project and Feedback locks, a changed Feedback (any revision change, including status) or edited plan is refused as `FEEDBACK_CONVERSION_STALE` and nothing is created, and a retry returns the same Work. The `Origin` link is a `project_feedback_work_origin` row (`Feedback` to `Work`, unique per pair); several Feedback records can origin-link to one Work as separate rows and the Feedback list shows `Origin of` provenance only, never a count, vote or priority. Conversion never writes the Feedback row: it stays undeleted, unarchived, same status and revision. The target Project is fixed to the Feedback's Project; cross-Project targets remain later work.

- **Feed (#363).** `Feed` is a read-only projection over the same Feedback and long-body Source master records (a Source counts only when its saved version has captured content). `packages/api/src/feedback-feed.ts` owns the strict row contract: a row's `id` and `projectId` are the master record's own and the schema has no like, comment, vote, read, notification, status-edit or priority field. The store port `FeedbackFeedStore` has only `read`, so filter and sort cannot write; `feedbackFeed` (oRPC) and `FeedbackAccess.feed` expose it. Filter and sort reuse Smart Collection vocabulary (`sourceType`, `conditions.status`) rather than a second query language; Smart Collections do not yet cover Feedback or Source records and have no sort, so the Feed applies `conditions.status` (Feedback `New` / `Reviewed` / `Archived`; a Source has no status and is excluded when it is set) and `Newest first` / `Oldest first` by time. Related Work/Decision is read from relations that exist today: Work-owned `work_relation` rows targeting the record and Evidence bindings sourced from it; the Feed creates none, so `Kökeni` conversion (#361/#362) appears once those relations exist. Identity is the Feedback channel or the Source provider. `Open Source Record` moves focus to the same master record element on the Feedback or Source surface; the Feed owns no detail view, never calls Source recheck or saves a Source version, and emits no Notification Center signal. UI labels: `Feed`, `Open Source Record`.

- **Evidence quality on the link (#362).** Kanıt niteliği, Kanıt Rolü and follow-up live on a sibling of the `Origin` link: `project_feedback_work_evidence` (migration `0119`, primary key and foreign key `origin_id` to `project_feedback_work_origin`) with `project_feedback_work_evidence_history`. This is the only Work–Feedback link that exists in the Feedback seam, and keying on it makes "per link, never copied to another Work" structural: the same Feedback has one row per Work. It is not stored on `evidence_binding`, because Feedback is not yet an Evidence source (spec 45 owns that), and not as columns on the `Origin` row, so conversion stays unchanged and history stays separate. `setFeedbackEvidence` (`FeedbackAccess.setEvidence`) is one strict command per (Feedback, Work): every key (`quality`, `role`, `followUp`) is optional, an omitted key is left as it was, `null` clears, and message, status, Work, priority, summary or score keys are refused. `quality` holds the seven separate fields (`Reported problem`, `Suggested solution`, `Current workaround`, `Impact severity`, `Usage frequency`, `Independence`, `Audience fit`), each blank or any value including `Unknown`. A founder marks a value `Founder interpretation` when the source did not state it; the stored entry then carries the actor alias (`accountActorAlias`, as in Contact merge) and the commit time, `Unknown` is never an interpretation, and an unchanged value keeps its author and time. `mergeLinkEvidence` in `packages/api/src/feedback.ts` is the only merge rule; nothing reads the original message, so nothing is extracted. `role` reuses the closed Evidence Role catalog (`Unspecified` default) as its own column and is never computed from, or used to compute, quality; there is no combined score or weight. `Follow up` / `Followed up` / `Outcome verified` is a nullable link-local column; the command cannot reach Feedback status, revision, Work or email sync, and the adapter writes only the two link tables. Changes keep a history row (revision, changed field names, actor alias, time), no message text. Writes need the `Origin` link (else `FEEDBACK_NOT_FOUND`), check `expectedRevision` of the link, and are refused in archived Projects. The Feedback list returns the link evidence on each `workOrigins` entry (blank defaults when no row exists). UI: `evidence-quality-panel.tsx` under `Origin of`, a keyboard and screen-reader accessible `details` form with labeled fields, `Unknown` buttons, a `Founder interpretation` checkbox per field, `Evidence Role`, `Follow-up` and `Save Evidence quality`. Kanıt Akışı, the general role editor and rendering of this link in Kanıt Akışı remain spec 45.

## Testing Decisions

- **What a good test is.** Tests observe Feedback through the public record-and-feed interface: create Feedback with original message/channel/time, optional Contact, convert-to-Work preview/commit, status independence from Work, niteliği vs original, Feed listing the same ids, Feed sort not writing status. They do not assert Drizzle shapes. Expected values are product rules (record remains after convert, unknown sender allowed, no social actions).
- **Seam (one).** Feedback — the product-facing Feedback record and Feed reading surface. Capture intake and Source storage are adapters/counterparts, not a second module under test.
- **Modules under test.** Feedback only. Contact merge, personal-data erase, Kanıt Akışı, Notification Center, and Source recheck are counterparts (“absent / not redefined”).
- **Prior art.** Same single-seam contract style as Account Access. Bind to [Kanıt akışı](../../prd/16-product-acceptance.md#uctan-uca-kabul-yolculuklari). Cloud tests must not use production message bodies.
- **Required counterparts.** Source-subtype behavior absent; Feedback is not a feature request, Work record, or social post; convert does not delete Feedback; Feed row is not a notification or universal-search hit type; likes/comments absent; Contact not required; Kanıt Rolü not derived from niteliği; Feed does not write Source version; Contact merge and personal-data erase absent.

- **#329 intake counterpart evidence.** `apps/server/src/features/research-sessions/server/research-sessions-database.test.ts` observes original message/channel/time, `New` status and owner-scoped reading from a Research Session conversion, preview without targets and same-key retry without duplicates. Feedback is a counterpart at that consuming seam; the full Feedback/Feed seam remains undelivered.

- **Research Evidence record-opening evidence.** `apps/server/src/features/research-sessions/server/research-sessions-database.test.ts` observes the owned archived original-message reader and foreign-account refusal through the consuming Research Sessions seam. `apps/web/src/features/research-sessions/ui/research-sessions-view.test.tsx` verifies explicit opening and cache suppression after a failed fresh read. These cover the Research Evidence record opening collaborator decision.

- **Feedback master record evidence (#360, Kanıt akışı journey).** `apps/server/src/features/feedback/server/feedback.test.ts` (DB-free) observes create with original message/channel/time/link and no Contact, trimmed-nothing-lost message, Source-subtype absence (no `sourceType`, recheck, snapshot or version field; Source-shaped, summary, comment, vote, like and reply input refused), and the status command touching only the Feedback store and refusing Work status, priority and Kanban fields. `feedback-database.test.ts` (PostgreSQL, skipped without `ACCOUNT_ACCESS_DATABASE_URL`) observes owner scoping, active same-Project attachment references, no `source` row on create and an unchanged Work row after `Reviewed` and `Archived`. `apps/web/src/features/feedback/ui/feedback-view.test.tsx` verifies the labels, verbatim original message, the three statuses and the absence of Work, social and Source controls. These bind the original-message-remains-the-record step of the Kanıt akışı journey.

- **Optional identity and Convert to Work evidence (#361, Kanıt akışı journey).** `feedback.test.ts` (DB-free) observes save without Contact, link and clear of Contact/Company, the identity command refusing status, message, Work and personal-data fields, the read-only preview (one Work, Target Project, title/description mapping, `Origin`, only `readConversionSource` called), refusal of `Feature`, confirm creating exactly one Work and leaving status and revision unchanged, same-key retry returning the same Work, and stale or edited/forged plans being refused without creating a Work. `feedback-database.test.ts` (PostgreSQL, skipped without `ACCOUNT_ACCESS_DATABASE_URL`) observes workspace-scoped identity (foreign Contact refused with no half-created Feedback), the real Work row (key, `Not Started`, description) with its `Origin` row, the Feedback row byte-identical after conversion, idempotent retry, stale refusal, owner scope and archived-Project refusal, and two Feedback records origin-linked to one Work without any vote or priority field. `feedback-view.test.tsx` verifies `Contact (optional)`/`Company (optional)` with `Unknown`, `Convert to Work`, the preview content, `Origin of` provenance and the absence of vote/priority text. Feed belongs to #363; niteliği is delivered by #362 below.

- **Feed evidence (#363, Kanıt akışı journey).** `apps/server/src/features/feedback/server/feedback-feed.test.ts` (DB-free) observes the same master ids with identity, time, attachments, project and related Work/Decision, filter and sort by source type, status and time, no write on a read-only store with rows unchanged, refusal of status, priority, like, vote, comment and recheck input, absence of social or notification keys on rows, and owner scoping. `apps/web/src/features/feedback/ui/feed-view.test.tsx` verifies the `Feed` and `Open Source Record` labels, the target element ids of the same master records and the absence of social, status-edit, notification and recheck controls. The PostgreSQL reader `feedback-feed-database.ts` is read-only by construction and has no PostgreSQL seam test yet; Convert to Work and Kanıt niteliği remain #361/#362.

- **Evidence quality evidence (#362, Kanıt akışı journey).** `feedback.test.ts` (DB-free) observes blank defaults that never block the relation, the seven separate fields beside a verbatim original message, `Founder interpretation` entries with author and time and `Unknown` never interpreted, no value extracted from the message (only the store's `setEvidence` is called), role independent of quality in both directions with no score or weight, quality and follow-up on one Work link absent from another Work link of the same Feedback, the three `Follow up` states leaving Feedback status, revision, message and Work untouched, unchanged values keeping author/time with no extra history, clearing with history, refusal of message, status, Work, priority, score and unknown values, and the missing link, foreign account and stale revision refusals. `feedback-database.test.ts` (PostgreSQL, skipped without `ACCOUNT_ACCESS_DATABASE_URL`) observes per-link rows with the Feedback and Work rows byte-identical, no row for the other link, role independence across commands, owner scope, revision conflict, missing Origin and archived Project refusal. `feedback-view.test.tsx` verifies the seven labels, `Unknown`, `Founder interpretation` with author and time, `Evidence Role`, the `Follow up` states, blank initial values and no score text, and the read-only mode.

## Out of Scope

- Geri Bildirimi özellik isteği, İş veya sosyal gönderi sayma.
- Contact birleştirme, kişisel veri silme, Kaynak yeniden kontrol / sürüm yaşamı.
- Kanıt Akışı yüzeyi ve genel Kanıt Rolü editörü (45).
- Birleşik Bildirim Merkezi, evrensel arama, inbox veya destek aracı.
- Herkese açık form, yorum, oy, çift yönlü requester konuşması, e-posta senkronu.
- Sosyal gönderi yaşam döngüsü, beğeni, yorum dizisi.
